# Changelog

## 1.5.0
- Boss markers on the dungeon maps: each boss's portrait where it stands, greyed with a tick once killed, the next one glowing. Hover for loot; click a boss in the side panel to flash its marker. In 14 dungeons (not yet in Shadowfang Keep, Deadmines, Scarlet Monastery and Zul'Farrak).
- Also includes 1.4.1 (that upload didn't go through): maps for the new Forever dungeons Ruins of Lordaeron, City of Dalaran, Excavation Site: Wetlands and The Drowned City.

## 1.4.1
- Maps for the new Forever dungeons in the download too: Ruins of Lordaeron, City of Dalaran, Excavation Site: Wetlands and The Drowned City (Icy Veins / Warcraft Tavern), and a labeled Hall of Thanes map.

## 1.4.0
- Dungeon maps for 19 dungeons: Ragefire Chasm through Stratholme, plus The Hall of Thanes. These are Wowhead's overhead maps. The Forever client has no dungeon maps at all, old dungeons included, so 1.3.0 showed "no map art" everywhere.

## 1.3.0
- Side panel can be dragged anywhere on the map (right-click its title to put it back). Collapsed, it's just a small title tab.
- Map search also finds dungeon maps the game files as "micro" or "orphan" maps (Hall of Thanes).
- Guide-map viewer for dungeons the game has no map for yet (the images aren't in the public download).

## 1.2.0
- Dungeon browser outside dungeons: every dungeon by level, full quest chains with prerequisites and follow-ups, quest givers, entrances, bosses and loot.
- Quest givers on zone maps, with waypoints that clear when you arrive.
- `/fdm dungeons`.

## Earlier
- Dungeon and raid maps on the normal world map, with floors and wings.
- Boss, quest and group tracking in the side panel.
